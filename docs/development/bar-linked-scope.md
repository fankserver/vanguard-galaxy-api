# Linked story/bar restoration probe

Prepare a fresh isolated `Full` sandbox with `-BarProbe -BarLinkedStory`, both independent bar author binaries and the exact reviewed API build. This cannot be combined with the cold bar sequence or consumer profile. The launcher enables story protection, mission identity and persistence, selects the linked branch from provenance, and validates the exact receipt plus committed native/API generation association.

The author declares an owned scripted mission and a persistent contact linked to its occurrence. It supplies no serializer or persistence callbacks. The probe activates the actual mission, places the contact once, saves and reloads, and checks automatic reconstruction of the active occurrence and contact. Every load waits for established native readiness.

To establish an independent vanilla baseline, ordinary additive policy and Unregister expose actual native patrons. The fixture records object identity/order/seats and verifies their complete native JSON representation, then re-registers the linked definition with exclusive policy without another Place call. Releasing only the story provider must refuse admission and restore those exact vanilla objects and content. Registration before reload restores the saved mission/contact without replacement placement. Loading the earlier empty save rejects future state; returning to the populated save restores it again.

This is a same-process linked-story and save-as/rollback test, not a cold missing-binary test or an API-created world-content test. It does not establish #21 owned-world reference reconstruction. Source review and host tests alone do not qualify native execution.
