# Restricted world probe phases

The world runner requires `world.enabled`, two registered independent authors and exactly one of `--vgmodapi-world-only` or `--vgmodapi-world-cold`. These flags do not authorize a native run. The isolated candidate additionally requires its verified authorization context. A reviewed exclusive launcher and preservation verification remain prerequisites.

Creation loads `fixture-a`, uses public author wrappers to create and find two empty sites, checks selected-system membership/order and global owned-ID uniqueness, and invokes ordinary save to `qa-owned-world`. It reads committed metadata through the integrity decoder and independently hashes the native file. Both inventory rows and their retained declarations must match. This read-only private decoder access is not the creation path.

Cold mode loads **the same canonical `qa-owned-world` slot**, not a renamed fixture. Before loading and after lookup/native membership checks, the decoded association must equal the saved creation receipt (slot, native hash, state hash, snapshot and two IDs). Moving a save or its sidecars to another path does not preserve canonical-slot authority. The launcher must establish a new process and bind its exact inputs to the preceding phase.

`world-created-generation.txt` and `world-cold-generation.txt` are seven-line paired-generation receipts. `owned-world.txt` distinguishes the creation and cold phases. Global `result.txt=PASS` alone is never world acceptance. Receipts do not prove interruption of in-progress native writes, unrelated-world property preservation, travel/arrival, story/bar ordering, migration, provider-loss controls, API absence or broader content behavior. Those remain required evidence, not waived scope.
